Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This page describes the cookies and similar technologies used by bussolacapital.it, in accordance with Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003) and the Italian Data Protection Authority's Guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021. The data controller is identified in the Privacy Policy. In case of discrepancies, the Italian version prevails.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on the user's device. Similar technologies, such as the browser's localStorage, can also store information on the device. They can be technical (needed for the website to work or to provide a service requested by the user, which do not require consent) or profiling / third-party analytics tools, which require prior consent.

2. What this website uses

This website does not use profiling cookies, statistics or analytics cookies, advertising pixels or any other tracking tool. Neither the website nor the hosting server sets cookies on your device.

The only information stored on your device is your language preference:

NameTypePurposeDuration
bussola-langlocalStorage, first-party, technicalRemembering the language you chose (Italian or English) so the website opens in the same language on your next visitUntil you clear your browser data

As this is a technical tool, strictly necessary to provide a feature you requested, no consent is required. For this reason the website does not display a cookie banner.

3. Third-party services

The website does not load resources from third-party servers: typefaces, images and videos are hosted on the website itself. When you browse its pages, your browser therefore communicates only with the hosting server (Netlify), as described in the Privacy Policy.

The website contains links to external websites (for example LinkedIn, the investors' websites and the press articles). These websites are not loaded until you click the link; once opened, their own cookie policies apply.

4. Managing cookies and stored data

You can delete the data stored by the website, including the language preference, at any time from your browser settings ("Clear browsing data" or equivalent). Instructions are available in the guides of the main browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.

5. Changes

If the website introduces tools that require consent in the future (for example statistics tools), this page will be updated and your consent will be requested before they are activated.
